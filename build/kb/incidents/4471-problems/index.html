The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. 7 April goes out as 4 July.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type what it says, and the form reads the date day first. Only through that form, at three tenants. A parser fix does not touch it.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, not by a swap: Tuesday in Lisbon, Monday in Toronto. It points to a time-zone conversion. The desk noted these reports and filed them under the parser by tenant.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges reports on subject line alone. All three problems come in under the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so the subject rule put them in one ticket.
